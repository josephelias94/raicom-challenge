  Rules

  Orders are processed in at order, with edits merged first. Each order goes
  through these in turn:
  1. If the country isn't US, it goes to PARTNER.
  2. If the ZIP is missing or its first three digits aren't in zones.json, it
     goes to PARTNER.
  3. A warehouse is eligible if it's enabled and has enough current stock for
     every SKU in the order. Orders are never split.
  4. Pick the lowest zone among eligible warehouses.
  5. On a tie, pick the highest cover, which is the smallest available ÷ 
     dailyVelocity across the order's SKUs.
  6. If still tied, use the priority WH-NJ, WH-GA, WH-NV, WH-TX.
  7. If no warehouse is eligible, it goes to PARTNER.

  A routed order then reduces that warehouse's stock. PARTNER has no stock to
  track.
